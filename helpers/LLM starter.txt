I'm using an ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 N16R8 (esp32-s3, 16 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM) in a platformio c++ project. It's a digital counter created to help with scoring Squash matches.
It supports multiple players in a tournament into multiple matches between each of them. The device itself has a 4-digit display with a colon on the front and OLED display with two ARGB LEDs on its sides on the rear of the device.
It's meant to be hanged on the rear wall of Squash court, so the players can see it through the glass, and the rear display is for people sitting on the bench in front of a court.
It is connected to the 4-button remote, powered by 18650 with a boost converter / charger. The ws2812b leds in digital digit display, 21 for each of 4 digits.
It uses OTA to flash the software from cLion. The SSD1306 is 128x64 display is a monochromatic OLED driven by I2C.

There are a total of 112 leds in the device:
- 21 for each of 4 digits - 3 per segment, 7 segments per digit
- 2 for the colon between the digits
- 2 for back display sides
- 24 on the status bar

Pinout:
GPIO03 – buzzer, active high (via MOSFET)
GPIO08 / 10 / 13 / 14 – remote inputs A / B / C / D (interrupt, INPUT_PULLDOWN)
GPIO18 – WS2812B data
GPIO04 – I2C SDA (SSD1306)
GPIO05 – I2C SCL (SSD1306)
GPIO06 – battery sense (ADC1, divider)

# Scoreboard Connect - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Scoreboard Connect is a Garmin Connect IQ watch app that remote-controls a Scoreboard display
(this project's hardware) over Bluetooth. This policy explains what data the app handles and where
it goes.

## Summary

The app does not collect, sell or share your data with us or with anyone else. It has no internet
access. Data moves only between your watch and the Scoreboard you pair it with, over Bluetooth,
and from there to the other players' watches connected to the same Scoreboard.

## Data the app handles

**Score and match control.** Your watch sends score and match commands (point, undo, start, next
game) to the Scoreboard and receives the current game state back.

**Player and team names.** The Scoreboard sends its list of players or teams to your watch so you
can pick who is playing. The names are entered on the Scoreboard by its owner. The watch keeps a
copy so the list opens quickly next time.

**Pairing.** When you pair, the watch and the Scoreboard agree on a key used to recognise each
other on later connections. The key and the Scoreboard's identifier are stored on your watch and on
that Scoreboard only.

**Heart rate (HeartSync).** While a match is running, your watch shares your current heart rate
with the Scoreboard, which passes it on to the other players' watches connected to it, so each
player can see the others' heart rate during the match. The Scoreboard keeps only the latest value
in memory and does not store it. You can turn HeartSync off in the app's settings; your watch then
neither sends your heart rate nor shows anyone else's.

**Activity recording.** If you record the match as an activity, the watch saves it (time, heart
rate, laps) like any built-in Garmin activity. It appears in your activity history and syncs to
Garmin Connect under Garmin's own privacy policy. The app does not receive or send that activity
anywhere.

**Settings.** Your app settings are stored on your watch.

## What we do not do

- The app has no internet permission and makes no network requests.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting.
- No location data is used.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

## The Scoreboard

The Scoreboard is a separate device owned by whoever installed it, for example a club. It uses
Wi-Fi only for its own setup page and firmware updates and does not upload match or player data
anywhere. Player names on it are managed by its owner.

## Deleting your data

Forgetting a Scoreboard in the app (Boards, pick the board, Forget) deletes its key and cached names
from your watch. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your watch. Recorded activities
are managed in Garmin Connect.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
